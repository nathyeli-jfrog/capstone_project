# HISTORICAL - Restricted Invitation Regulations (2020)
**Status:** EXPIRED / OBSOLETE (Do Not Apply)
**Valid Dates:** 2020-01-01 to 2022-12-31

## Article 1. Outdated Permitted Amounts
This procedure was mandatory for acquisitions between $15,001 USD and $99,999 USD.

## Article 2. Obsolete Rule of Two Vendors
Under the 2020 regulations, only 2 vendors were required for invitation procedures.
