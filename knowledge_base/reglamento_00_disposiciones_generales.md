# General Procurement Provisions
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 onwards

## Article 1. Purpose of Purchases
All acquisitions must be strictly linked to business operations and operationally justified.

## Article 2. Universally Prohibited Goods (STRICT RULE)
Regardless of the amount, urgency, or procurement procedure used, the purchase of the following goods with institutional funds is **strictly prohibited**:
- Alcoholic beverages (including tequila, wine, beer, etc.).
- Corporate or personal gifts.
- Luxury vehicles.
- Designer furniture.

No exceptions are valid for Article 2. If a requirement includes these goods, it must be rejected immediately.
