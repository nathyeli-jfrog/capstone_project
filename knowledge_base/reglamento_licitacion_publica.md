# Open Public Tender Regulations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 onwards

## Article 1. Permitted Amounts
Any institutional purchase that is equal to or greater than $200,000 USD must inevitably undergo an Open Public Tender.

## Article 2. Permitted Use and Goods
Used for high-impact acquisitions:
- Network infrastructure, massive servers, and data centers.
- Comprehensive institutional remodeling projects.
- Multi-year service contracts.
