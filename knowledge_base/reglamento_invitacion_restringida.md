# Restricted Invitation (Minimum 3 Vendors) Regulations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 onwards

## Article 1. Permitted Amounts
This procedure is mandatory for any acquisition whose total estimated amount is between $50,000 USD and $199,999 USD.

## Article 2. Permitted Use and Goods
This procedure is designed for standardized business purchases:
- Corporate cafeteria supplies (coffee, dispensers).
- Renewal of software licenses and standard technological equipment (laptops for employees).
- Basic ergonomic furniture.

## Article 3. Rule of Three Vendors
It is strictly mandatory to submit 3 different vendors for evaluation and verify that none are on the blocked vendors list (blacklist).
