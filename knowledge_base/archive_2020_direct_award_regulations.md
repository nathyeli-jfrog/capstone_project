# HISTORICAL - Direct Award Procedure Regulations (2020)
**Status:** EXPIRED / OBSOLETE (Do Not Apply)
**Valid Dates:** 2020-01-01 to 2022-12-31

## Article 1. Outdated Permitted Amounts
The Direct Award procedure applies exclusively when the total estimated purchase amount is from $1 USD up to a maximum of $15,000 USD.

## Article 2. Obsolete Conditions
Purchases above $15,000 USD in this era required explicit authorization from the regional board.
