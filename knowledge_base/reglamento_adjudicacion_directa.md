# Direct Award Procedure Regulations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 onwards

## Article 1. Permitted Amounts
The Direct Award procedure applies exclusively when the total estimated purchase amount is from $1 USD up to a maximum of $49,999 USD.

## Article 2. Permitted Use and Goods
This procedure is only allowed for:
- Minor office supplies and daily stationery.
- Justified emergency operational purchases.
- Acquisition of exclusive brand goods or patents where only one vendor exists in the market.
